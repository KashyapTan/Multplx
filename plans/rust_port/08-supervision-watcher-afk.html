Overview

This portion replaces the supervision chain that keeps a broker responsive without an idle model loop.

The chain crosses status writers, queue locks, native transitions, hook transports, watcher processes, checkpoint timeouts, AFK ownership, and sub-supervisor injection.

Continuity gate

Do not switch any primary hook to Rust until a complete end-to-end chain proves that actionable events are durably queued before detector state advances and that quiet or failed cycles cannot leave work unsupervised.

Implementation steps

  1. Port the command-policy parser once.Replace both JavaScript policies with one narrow Rust tokenizer and command-position analyzer, preserving accepted grammar and stable reason codes.
  2. Port hook transports.Read payloads once, enforce primary scope, bound input, preserve stdout/stderr and exit behavior, and fail open or closed exactly as each harness contract requires.
  3. Port status reporting and MCP.Keep one status vocabulary and binding owner, append first, journal best-effort, nudge only a verified live watcher, and keep JSON-RPC framing exact.
  4. Port native transition handling.Use typed transitions, pause precedence, wedge tracking, journal observation, and queue-before-state publication.
  5. Port watcher scanning.Preserve signal order, current-state proof, no-verb handling, stale handling, authenticated check execution, bounded capture, heartbeat, AFK behavior, and triage absorption.
  6. Port arm and checkpoint ownership.Track the watcher as an owned child, verify its lock and beacon, reconcile timeout, preserve exit 124, and never rely on a detached shell child.
  7. Port turn-end adapters.Preserve Claude and Codex blocking semantics, Pi bounded follow-up, Cursor transport, budget guards, and auto-arm ownership.
  8. Port AFK transfer and return.Keep one durable flag, exact sub-supervisor identity, marked injections, batched escalation, stale-artifact recovery, and return catch-up.
  9. Cut over one full primary harness at a time.Empirically verify its complete hook and supervision chain before enabling the next harness.
